# reel-visuals

Working references, all rendered and checked: `style_lab.html` (hook, parallax, route), `map_lab.html` (India -> Kerala), `people_lab.html` (people + objects). Copy from them; don't start blank.

## 1. The first 3 seconds (see `growth/log.md`: 61% skip on a dark, static opening)

- Frame 0 is bright and saturated (warm gradient, rays) and already moving. No fade from black.
- A **slam**: the key word or number scales in from about 3x with motion blur (feGaussianBlur stdDeviation on one axis), overshoots, a shockwave ring, a particle burst, and a short decaying camera shake.
- Supporting text **whips** in (fast translate, skewX and horizontal blur), then words **drop** one per beat with squash and stretch.
- A big hook card stays on screen for 0-3 s. Malayalam words stack vertically when they don't fit on one line (check, they overlap easily).

## 2. Cinematic grammar

| tool | how |
| --- | --- |
| Letterbox | 90 px bars slide in over 0.8 s |
| Handheld drift | camera += sin(t*.9)*6 + sin(t*2.3)*2, cos(t*.7)*5 |
| Slow push | scale 1 -> 1.2 over the shot, eased, towards the subject's face |
| Punch-in | +6% scale for 0.4 s on the big moment |
| Rack focus | background group under a blur filter; animate stdDeviation 2 -> 9 when the moment lands |
| Speed ramp | remap local time: normal, then 0.45x through the key action, then normal |
| Light | warm pool (radial gradient), light beam polygon, dust motes drifting in it, flickering bulb, bokeh dots (blurred circles) |
| Cuts | iris (expanding clipPath circle), whip pan (1300 px slide + 70 px horizontal blur on both shots), white flash (0.18 s) |
| Vignette | radial gradient to 0.6-0.7 black at the edges, always on |

## 3. Layered parallax scenes

Build 5-8 depth layers (sky, sun, far hills, haze, mid, water, subject, foreground). Each layer moves by `cam * depth` and zooms by `(zoom-1) * depth`, so near things slide faster. Add secondary motion everywhere: palms sway with fronds lagging, sails breathe, boats bob and roll, water shimmer, birds flapping. A still layer looks dead.

## 4. Maps (India, Kerala)

- Data: `lib/maps/india_kerala.js` -> `window.MAP` (generated by `tools/mapgen.mjs`).
  - `MAP.india`, `MAP.land` (Natural Earth land around India), `MAP.states[]` {name, d, cx, cy, box}, `MAP.kerala`, `MAP.districts[]` (14, with Malayalam names in `ml`).
  - `MAP.xy(lon, lat)` -> [x, y] in the same map space (Mercator).
- Boundaries are the official Survey of India ones (datameet/maps, MIT; `tools/mapdata/LICENSE-datameet.txt`). Never swap in a world map that draws India's borders differently; viewers notice.
- Look: dark teal sea with drifting lines, land slab with a 12-layer extrusion and soft shadow, warm states, Kerala in green lifted as its own slab with a glowing outline.
- Camera: one group, `translate(540 960) rotate(r) scale(z) translate(-cx -cy)`; interpolate zoom in log space. Rotate about 26 degrees when on Kerala so it stands upright and fills the 9:16 frame.
- Strokes use `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`. Labels, pins and routes live in a screen-space group (project with the camera) so they stay the same size.
- Turn off big blur filters (shadow, coast glow) once zoom > 2.2, or frames take minutes and the render times out.
- To regenerate: `npm i mapshaper d3-geo` somewhere, then `NODE_PATH=<that>/node_modules node tools/mapgen.mjs`. Other states or district sets: filter `India-Districts-2011Census.shp` from datameet/maps (`Survey-of-India-Index-Maps/Boundaries`).

## 5. People (lib/people.js v2)

- `p.S.reachN / reachF = { x, y, w }` - two-bone IK: the hand goes to a point in the parent's coordinates; `w` blends from the pose. Arm reach is about 180 units x scale (0.62 x height x S.s); keep targets inside it or the arm locks straight.
- `p.hold(group, 'N'|'F')` - the object rides in the hand and stays upright; tilt it with your own inner group (pouring). `p.release(side)` then `other.hold(group, side)` passes it between people (the tea glass).
- `p.handIn(group, side)` and a `ptIn(marker, group)` helper give world positions of a hand or of a point on a held object (stream from a glass lip, a phone's light on the face).
- Acting: `headTilt` (nod or sip back), `look`, `surprise` 0-1 (wide eyes, brows up, "o" mouth), `frown`, `smile`, `lean` (anticipation before a reach, recoil after a shock). Breathing is automatic.
- Beats that read: notice (head turns), anticipate (lean), act (reach), react (surprise with a punch-in). Every object a character uses must be touched by a hand; nothing floats.
- Side view: keep a raised near arm forward of the face so it doesn't cover it.
- **2.5D look (v3):** limbs and torso are cylinder-shaded, head and hands sphere-shaded, with occlusion at shoulders, joints and neck, a soft contact shadow and eyes with an iris and a catchlight. Set `rim` to the scene's back-light colour (cool blue at night, warm amber indoors) and `rimOpacity` about .35-.45. Kerala dress: `lower: 'mundu'` (kasavu border follows the legs), `mustache: true`.
- Free 3D rigged characters on GitHub (CC0 GLB packs) are all low-poly, the look the owner rejected; we stay with this shaded SVG rig.
- Scale: a person should be 55-70% of the frame height in a medium shot (S.s about 2.5); small people in a big room read as empty.

## 7. Installed GitHub skills (use them for machines and "how it works")

| skill | use it for | notes here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `teardown` (elvarofficial/teardown, MIT) | Real 3D machines that work: watch movements, engines, gears. Studio light, cutaways, the part doing the work opens up; one input drives every part with real ratios | `cd .claude/skills/teardown && npm install` once (never `playwright install`). Run capture with `CHROMIUM=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`. Its overlay is laid out for 1600x900: for reels, render the machine at 1080x1920 and put our own Malayalam labels on top. SwiftShader works but is slow. |
| `anatomy` (wheresryan22/anatomy, MIT) | Isometric exploded "anatomy" figures: many small precise parts, four-tone shading, parts coming apart in order | Examples build with `node examples/<name>/build.mjs` (`--light` for a light theme; light reads better in a feed). |
| `hyperframes` (+ `-cli`, `-registry`; heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0) | Its registry of about 450 ready blocks (transitions, titles, kinetic type, camera moves) and its motion references | Never run its telemetry (`events`, `feedback`). We keep our own pipeline; borrow blocks and ideas. |

Qwen3-TTS (QwenLM, Apache-2.0) was checked and not installed: it speaks 10 languages, none of them Malayalam, and needs a GPU. ElevenLabs stays the voice.

## 6. Checks before rendering

- Screenshot 6-10 moments with `PAGE=x.html node tools/shots.js outdir t1 t2 ...` and look at them as a contact sheet.
- No overlapping Malayalam words, nothing important in the top 230 px, bottom 400 px or right 140 px.
- `tools/render.js` screenshots have a 180 s timeout; if frames are slow, look for blur filters on huge shapes first.
