# The 1924 flood: Munnar's vanished train (Video 10)

**Emotion:** awe that turns into loss, then pride (Kerala remembers, and stood back up). **Share reason:** every Malayali family has heard "തൊണ്ണൂറ്റിയൊമ്പതിലെ വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കം" from grandparents, and 2018 brought it back.

## Accuracy notes

- "India's first monorail" is how Wikipedia and Kerala Tourism describe it. By 1924 it had been converted to a narrow-gauge line, so the script says it *began* as a monorail.
- Duration of rain: sources range from a three-day cyclone (16-18 July) to three weeks. The script says "ദിവസങ്ങളോളം" (for days).
- Left out: the Mullaperiyar dam breach story (folk belief, not established), a death toll (no reliable figure found), the Munnar 2,023 mm figure (single source), and Gandhi's fundraising (true, about Rs 6,000+, but amounts vary and it would push the reel past 45 s; keep for the caption).
- No invented people or dialogue. The "everyone remembered it in 2018" line reflects the widespread "worst since 1924" framing.
