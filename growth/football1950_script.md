# Video 17: India qualified for the World Cup... and never went (1950), English

**Our way:** small cause -> giant effect in the first breath (India qualified for the 1950 World Cup and stayed home; 76 years later it has still never played one). Then how: Brazil 1950, every Asian rival withdraws, the barefoot team of 1948, FIFA offers to pay, India says no. The popular story (FIFA demanded boots). A silence beat. The twist: boots weren't the reason; their own star said the Olympics was everything. Payoff: FIFA turned down India's 1954 entry. Callback: the World Cup "didn't seem big enough".
**Emotion:** "wait, what?" turning into regret.
**Share reason:** almost every Indian has heard "India didn't go because they had no boots". This busts that myth, which people send to the friend who repeats it. (No CTA in the reel.)
**Hook card (0-3 s):** QUALIFIED. NEVER WENT.
**Length:** about 104 English words. The maglev lesson says about 95 words for 35-45 s at Roger's pace, so expect about 45-50 s raw and sync with a light tempo (1.05-1.1).

## ElevenLabs paste block

```
1950. India qualified for the football World Cup... and never went. Seventy-six years on, it still hasn't played one.

Brazil was hosting. In Asia, every rival pulled out. India was in, without kicking a ball.

This team played barefoot. At the 1948 London Olympics, most of them faced France with bandaged feet.

FIFA offered to pay most of the trip. India still said no.

The story everyone tells? FIFA demanded boots, and India refused.

...

But boots weren't the reason. Their star defender, Sailen Manna, later said: "For us, the Olympics was everything."

FIFA didn't forget. India's entry for 1954 was turned down.

All because, in 1950, the World Cup didn't seem big enough.
```

## Accuracy notes

- **Was there a FIFA barefoot rule?** Sources disagree (some say FIFA brought one in after 1948, others say there was none in 1950). The script never says whether the rule existed; it says the boots story is "the story everyone tells" and that boots "weren't the reason", which the sources and Manna agree on.
- **Manna quote:** reported by The Quint and myKhel, attributed to an SI interview; the wording is consistent across reports. On screen, credit it "Sailen Manna, as reported". He was India's star defender (he captained India in 1951-52); some sources call him the likely 1950 captain, so the script says "star defender", not "captain".
- **"FIFA offered to pay most of the trip":** several outlets say FIFA agreed to bear a major part of the travel cost (some say Brazil/the organisers). Don't name the amount.
- **"Every rival pulled out":** Burma, Indonesia and the Philippines withdrew from India's qualifying group (Burma's withdrawal sealed it in Aug 1949). Keep "in Asia" loose; don't claim it was the whole continent's slot list.
- **1948 Olympics:** lost to France in the first round; most of the side played barefoot or with bandaged feet (one account: 8 barefoot, 3 in boots). The score is widely given as 2-1, but this search didn't confirm it, so the script gives no score.
- **"Never played one":** true for the senior men's team; India played the U-17 World Cup in 2017 (as hosts). Keep "the World Cup", not "any FIFA tournament".
- **1954:** FIFA refusing the entry comes from Kapadia's book and news reports; it is widely repeated but rests on few primary sources.
- No faces of real players. No Olympic rings or FIFA logos.
