# Video 15: The war that should have lifted gold (gold price fall, Nov 2026)

**Our way:** small cause -> giant effect in the first breath (a war far away, and a Kerala pavan drops by more than ₹8,000 in a week, the opposite of what everyone expects). Then how: the January record, then oil, inflation, the US rate hike and the dollar, rising step by step. A silence beat. The twist: AI shares crash and people sell their gold, not because gold is bad but because it is the quickest thing to sell. Payoff, then a callback: the war that should have lifted gold is what pulled it down.
**Emotion:** surprise ("wait, war made gold *cheaper*?"), then "now I get it".
**Share reason:** every Malayali family watches the pavan rate; "should we buy now?" is a group-chat question. This reel explains why in 40 seconds.
**Hook card (0-3 s):** യുദ്ധം... എന്നിട്ടും സ്വർണ്ണം ഇടിഞ്ഞു! (with ₹1,00,920 → ₹92,080 under it)
**Length:** about 85 Malayalam words, aimed at 40-45 s raw.

## Accuracy notes

- **Re-check the Kerala numbers on the day the voice is recorded.** The ₹1,00,920 -> ₹92,080 figures come from rate-aggregator sites, not AKGSMA or a jeweller board; the rate changes daily. If the weekly drop is no longer above ₹8,000, change line 2 (the hook card follows it).
- **"A third lower" is the world (US dollar) price**: record $5,589 (some sources give $5,595 or $5,602) to about $3,514, roughly 36%. The Indian price has fallen much less because of the 15% import duty and the weak rupee, which is why line 3 doesn't say "in Kerala". Don't put "36%" next to the pavan rate on screen.
- **The rate hike is expected, not done.** Markets priced ~96% odds of a 25 bp hike at the 8-9 Dec Fed meeting (CME FedWatch, 20 Nov). The script says markets are sure, not that the Fed has raised rates. If the Fed has already acted when this posts, change line 7.
- **"4-5%" is US money-market fund yields**, not an Indian bank rate. "ബാങ്കിലിട്ടാൽ" simplifies it; on screen, label it "US SAVINGS ~4-5%".
- **Which war:** sources describe the US-Israel conflict with Iran pushing Brent to about $112 in October. The script just says "a war" to stay neutral and accurate; Brent has since come down about 21% from its peak, but hike bets stayed because inflation is broad.
- **Forced selling is one analyst's explanation (David Meger, via CNBC) plus InvestingLive**, widely repeated but still an analyst view. The script presents it as what happened to some investors, not the only cause.
- Several reasons-pages are broker/blog sites; the script only uses causes that CNBC, Kitco or The National also report. Nothing here is investment advice, and the reel makes no prediction about where gold goes next.
