# Video 22: A small studio in a father's garage... today at the door of ₹1,000 crore (Namit Malhotra, DNEG and Ramayana)

**Owner's pick (25 Nov 2026):** "Ramayana near ₹1,000 cr" from the 4 news options.
**Our way:** small cause -> giant effect in the first breath (a tiny studio in a garage -> India's ₹1,000-crore film). Then how: 1995, Mumbai, 19-year-old Namit Malhotra starts a video editing studio in his father's garage; it grows into Prime Focus; 2014, it buys London's Double Negative (DNEG), the Oscar-winning VFX house of Interstellar and Dune. Silence. Twist: the same Namit produced Ramayana, the biggest budget in Indian cinema. Payoff: 20 days, ₹973 crore in India alone. Callback: the garage dream, now right next to ₹1,000 crore.
**Why now:** Ramayana Part 1 (released 5 Nov 2026) is ₹27 crore short of ₹1,000 crore India net after day 20 (Sacnilk, 25 Nov).
**Emotion:** "wait, from a garage?!", then pride (an Indian who bought a Hollywood Oscar studio).
**Hook card (0-3 s):** ഒരു ഗാരേജ്... ₹1,000 കോടി!

## Accuracy notes

- **Box office:** ₹973 crore is Sacnilk's India net after day 20 (24 Nov). Trackers differ (Bollywood Hungama ₹926 cr, Wikipedia list ₹890 cr), so the screen says "INDIA NET · 20 DAYS · SACNILK". The voice says only "near ₹1,000 crore", never that it has crossed it. If it crosses before posting, change the payoff line to "ആയിരം കോടി കടന്നു" and the counter.
- **Not "first":** Pushpa 2 and Dhurandhar: The Revenge already crossed ₹1,000 crore net, so no "first film" claim is made.
- **"Biggest budget in Indian cinema":** based on the producer's own estimate (about $500 million, over ₹4,000 crore for both parts); the screen says "REPORTED".
- **Age 19:** born April 1976, so 18-19 in 1995; the voice says "പത്തൊമ്പതുകാരൻ" (19). The garage studio was Video Workshop; it became Prime Focus after a 1997 merger, so the voice says "it grew into Prime Focus".
- **DNEG Oscars:** the Oscars named are only ones DNEG certainly won (Interstellar, Dune). No total count (sources say 6, 7 or 8 depending on the year).
- **Visuals:** no film posters, stills or trailers (copyright); only Commons photos of the people and a generic statuette, no Academy logo.
