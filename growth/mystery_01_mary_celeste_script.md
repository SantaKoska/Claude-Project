## Accuracy notes
- "Two men boarded": Deveau and Wright (some accounts add a third). Voice says two.
- No hailing / shouting is claimed; the voice says only that no one was at the wheel or on deck.
- The last log entry: the voice gives only date and place ("nothing special"), no invented wording.
- The stains: sources differ on deck vs railing; one says rust. Voice: "red marks on the deck, a stain like blood on a sword... not blood".
- Nine empty barrels: left out (found at unloading in Genoa; confusing in a 60-s part).
- "Marie" vs "Mary": Doyle used "Marie Celeste".
- The breakfast myth is credited to "later stories", not to Doyle (sources do not show it in his text).
- No blood or bodies shown. Bloodshed may be spoken about only where sourced.
