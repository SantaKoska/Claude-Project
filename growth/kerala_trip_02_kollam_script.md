# Kerala Yatra, episode 2/14: Kollam

**Style:** narrator and navigator (see the `kerala-yatra` skill). Calm "നമ്മൾ" voice; each stop = how we get there -> name -> what we see -> one fact or tip.
**Language:** Malayalam voice; English names, distances and chips on screen.
**Length:** about 120 Malayalam words, about 75-80 s raw, synced to about 65 s.
**Opening card:** KERALA YATRA · EP 2/14 · KOLLAM (opens on the Kerala map at Varkala, where episode 1 ended).

## Accuracy notes

- **Lighthouse height** is given as 135 ft or 144 ft by different sources, so the script gives no height. Visiting hours also differ by source; none are given.
- **Portuguese fort:** sources mention the ruins; the script doesn't name the fort or give its year.
- **Munroe Island name:** the script doesn't explain the name (sources disagree on the colonel's first name).
- **Sasthamcotta:** "drinking water for Kollam" is from the Ramsar sheet; the number of people served varies by source, so none is given.
- **Jatayu:** dimensions differ online and aren't in the search results, so only "the world's largest bird sculpture" and the cable car are used. Fares and hours vary; none given.
- **Thenmala by train:** sources disagree on the nearest station, so the voice says only "east, to the hills" and the screen shows bus/taxi.
- **Times:** spoken times are rounded from OSRM drive times and typical train times (Varkala-Kollam about 30 min; Kollam-Munroe Thuruthu about 20 min per Veena World).
