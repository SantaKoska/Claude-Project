# Kerala Yatra, episode 3/14: Pathanamthitta

**Style:** narrator and navigator (see the `kerala-yatra` skill). Calm "നമ്മൾ" voice; each stop = how we get there -> name -> what we see -> one fact or tip.
**Language:** Malayalam voice; English names, distances and chips on screen.
**Length:** 106 Malayalam words (EP 2: 95 words = 62.6 s raw), so about 70 s raw, synced to about 62-65 s.
**Opening card:** KERALA YATRA · EP 3/14 · PATHANAMTHITTA (opens on the Kerala map at Thenmala, where episode 2 ended).
**Thread:** the Pamba river ties the last three stops together (Perunthenaruvi flows into it, Maramon sits on its sandbank, Aranmula on its bank).

## Accuracy notes

- **Gavi left out:** it needs a forest pass (limited vehicles a day) and is a 3 h detour off the road order; its access is also restricted at times during the Sabarimala season.
- **Sabarimala** is named only as the district's identity and the current season; no visit, rules or crowd numbers.
- **Konni:** no elephant rides or fees mentioned (fees vary; rides are an ethics question). Opening days come only from a third-party site, so none are given.
- **Adavi:** prices differ by source (Rs 400-500), so none are given.
- **Perunthenaruvi:** height is given as 60-300 ft by different sources, so no height is used.
- **Maramon:** said as "one of Asia's largest" (sources range from "Asia's biggest" to "one of the largest"); no attendance number or founding year (1895 vs 1896).
- **Aranmula kannadi:** made only by a few craftsman families in Aranmula (GI tag 2005); the herb/myth stories and the "300 years" claim are not used. The regatta is a ritual offering, not a race, so the voice says "വള്ളംകളി" without "competition".
- **Times:** spoken times are rounded from OSRM drive times; bus legs rounded up.
