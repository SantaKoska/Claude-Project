# Video 18: One mask came off... ₹48 crore of tickets a month early (Avengers: Doomsday)

**Our way:** small cause -> giant effect in the first breath (a mask came off on a stage, and a month before release India has bought ₹48 crore of tickets). Then how: 2019 Endgame, Iron Man dies, India's ticket frenzy; July 2024 Comic-Con, a villain in a green cloak; the mask comes off. Silence. Twist: it's Robert Downey Jr, Iron Man returning as the villain. Payoff: the new advance-booking record and Kerala's 12:01 AM first show. Callback to the mask.
**Emotion:** fan excitement, "wait, what?" at the reveal.
**Share reason:** every Marvel fan in Kerala is booking or talking about it this week; a quick "why is everyone going crazy" to send to the friend group. (No CTA.)
**Hook card (0-3 s):** ഒരു മുഖംമൂടി... ₹48 കോടി!
**Length:** about 85 Malayalam words, about 50 s raw, synced to about 40 s.

## Accuracy notes

- **₹48 crore** is a trade-tracker estimate (Sacnilk and others), not an official studio figure; trackers range ₹46.5-52.4 crore by cut-off time. The script says "48 crore" as reported by Onmanorama; on screen label it "ADVANCE BOOKINGS (TRACKER ESTIMATE)".
- **"A month before release":** bookings opened about 25-30 days before the 17 December release (sources differ), so "a month before" is a fair round figure.
- **18 tickets per second** was BookMyShow's reported peak rate for Endgame in 2019, not an average.
- **Kerala:** the Kerala-specific figures differ by tracker (₹1.09-2.57 crore), so no Kerala number is used; only the Thiruvananthapuram 12:01 am first-day first show.
- **No likenesses or Marvel designs:** no face of Downey, no copy of the Doctor Doom mask or Iron Man suit, no Marvel or BookMyShow logos. Generic mask, tickets, stage, map.
