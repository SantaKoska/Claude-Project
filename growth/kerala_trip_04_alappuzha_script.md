# Kerala Yatra, episode 4/14: Alappuzha

**Style:** narrator and navigator (see the `kerala-yatra` skill). Calm "നമ്മൾ" voice; each stop = how we get there -> name -> what we see -> one fact.
**Language:** Malayalam voice; English names, distances and chips on screen.
**Length:** 111 Malayalam words with the series intro (EP 3: 124 words = 66.9 s synced), so about 75 s raw, synced to about 62-65 s.
**Opening:** the space intro (Earth -> India -> Kerala -> 14 districts; Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta done, Alappuzha highlighted), then down to Aranmula, where episode 3 ended.

**Thread:** water. The trip goes from a palace mural to the sea, then onto the backwaters, and ends on a houseboat in Kuttanad, facing Kottayam across Vembanad.

## Accuracy notes

- **Mural size:** sources differ on the area (Kerala Tourism says 49 sq m, Wikipedia about 14 sq m), so the voice gives no size and says "one of the largest murals in Kerala".
- **Ambalappuzha:** the temple's age is given differently by sources (15th-17th century vs 790 AD), and the Guruvayur story is a legend, so neither is used. No timings.
- **Pier age:** sources say 137, 140 or 150 years, so the voice just says "old pier". The lighthouse date (1862) is firm, so "more than 150 years" is used for the lighthouse only.
- **Nehru Trophy:** usually the second Saturday of August, but the 2025 race was on 30 August, so the voice says only "every August".
- **Kuttanad:** depth below sea level is given as 1.2-3 m depending on the source, so no number. "Recognised by the United Nations" = FAO (a UN agency) GIAHS, 2013.
- **Houseboat leg:** no time is given (houseboat trips vary from hours to overnight); on the map this leg should be drawn over the water, not along the OSRM road.
- **Bus times:** rounded up from OSRM drive times (39 and 37 min) to "an hour" for buses with stops. No fares.
- **Left out:** Marari Beach (north, would break the road order towards Kottayam); Pathiramanal island (boat-only, seasonal).
