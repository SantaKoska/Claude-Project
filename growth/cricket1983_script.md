# Video 16: The passes that moved cricket to India (1983 -> today)

**Our way:** small cause -> giant effect in the first breath (a few passes refused at Lord's, and world cricket's power ends up in India). Then how: the 1983 final, the refusal, the joint bid with Pakistan, the 1987 World Cup leaving England. A silence beat. The twist: the board that won the 1983 World Cup had no money to reward its own players; Lata Mangeshkar sang for free to pay them. Payoff: today India takes more than a third of ICC revenue. Callback to the refused passes.
**Emotion:** pride, plus "wait, really?" at the twist.
**Share reason:** every Indian cricket fan knows 1983; almost nobody knows the passes story or the Lata concert. It's a "did you know this about 1983" moment people send to friends (no CTA in the reel).
**Hook card (0-3 s):** ഒരു "നോ"... ക്രിക്കറ്റ് ഇന്ത്യയുടേതായി!
**Length:** about 100 Malayalam words, aimed at 55-60 s raw, synced to about 45 s (same tempo method as the gold reel).

## Accuracy notes

- **How many passes, and who refused, is disputed.** Sources say two extra passes (Quint), four complimentary tickets (Dawn), or passes for the High Commissioner and his wife (Scroll); the refuser is given as the English board/Lord's officials or the ICC. The script says "a few passes" and "the answer was no" without naming who refused. Don't put a number of passes or "MCC said no" on screen.
- **Cause and effect:** the passes are the trigger every source tells, but the 1987 bid also rode on India's new standing after 1983 and Asian boards' wish to break England's hold. The hook ("that one no...") is the widely told version; Salve himself described the snub as the spark. Keep it framed as a trigger, not the only cause.
- **"No money":** based on a 1983 squad member's account (Sunil Valson: the board said "we don't have money") and the widely reported Lata Mangeshkar fundraiser. The concert (Delhi, about ₹20 lakh raised, ₹1 lakh per player) is reported by many outlets; venue and total differ slightly. The script says only "she sang without charging" and "each player got one lakh", the parts all sources agree on.
- **"More than a third"** is the BCCI's 38.5% share of ICC distributions in the 2024-27 cycle, not of all cricket revenue worldwide. On screen, label it "ICC REVENUE SHARE 2024-27".
- **Nobody's likeness:** show no faces of Kapil Dev, Salve, Lata Mangeshkar or players; use objects (pass, scoreboard, ball, microphone, trophy silhouette not copying the real Prudential trophy design closely).
- 1987 World Cup (Reliance Cup) was co-hosted by India and Pakistan: the first World Cup held outside England. India lost in the semi-final, so the script doesn't claim India won it.
