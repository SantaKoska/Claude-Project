## English

1. The rain turned red... why?
2. In July 2001, red rain fell in Changanassery, Kerala.
3. It went on for about two months.
4. At first, it was said a meteor might have exploded.
5. Later, a report said algae spores gave the rain its colour.
6. But some scientists think something else: a comet that broke apart.
7. Which is right? The evidence is still disputed.
8. What do you think? Comment, and send this to a friend!

## Facts and sources

- Began 25 July 2001 and recurred for about two months. Source: [Louis & Kumar, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0310120)
- First linked to a meteor by officials, later attributed by a CESS/TBGRI report to algae spores. Source: same paper.
- Cometary origin: Godfrey Louis and P. Kumar, a minority view. Source: [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0310120)

## Avoided

- "aliens" or "proof of life".
- Claiming the comet theory is proven or the algae explanation is final. Both are shown as views.
