# Kerala Yatra, episode 6/14: Idukki

**Style:** narrator and navigator (`kerala-yatra` skill). Calm "നമ്മൾ" voice; each stop = how we get there -> name -> what we see -> one fact or tip.
**Length:** 110 Malayalam words with the series intro (EP 5: 119 words = 62.5 s synced), so about 58-60 s synced.
**Opening:** space intro (districts 1-5 done, Idukki highlighted), then down to Illikkal Kallu, where episode 5 ended.
**Opening card:** KERALA YATRA · EP 6/14 · IDUKKI. Chip: HILLS · MIST.
**Thread:** up into the high ranges: meadows, a forest lake, a dam between two hills, tea, and the Nilgiri tahr; then down west to Ernakulam.

## Accuracy notes
- Dam height: 168.91 m (Wikipedia); Kerala Tourism says 550 ft (~168 m). Voice: 168 m.
- Idukki Dam visiting: open hours differ by source and visits are sometimes restricted; the voice gives no timing or "open" claim.
- Thekkady boat: departure times and tariffs differ by source (KTDC vs forest department); none in the voice. Wildlife sightings are typical, not guaranteed ("can see").
- Eravikulam: closure dates are the usual pattern (sometimes extended to mid-April); voice says "February, March".
- Nilgiri tahr: endangered (IUCN). Neelakurinji (next bloom ~2030) left out: no firm source.
- Bus times rounded up from OSRM drive times for ghat roads; no fares.
- Left out: Ramakkalmedu, Kalvari Mount, Mattupetty, Anamudi (inside Eravikulam; possible chip later).
