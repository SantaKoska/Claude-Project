## Accuracy notes

- No public source names a Malayali developer on GTA 6, so the script does not claim one. The Kerala link is geographic ("a night's journey from Kerala"), which is true and honest.
- The Vice Beach claim comes from media reports, not Rockstar, so the script says "റിപ്പോർട്ടുകൾ പ്രകാരം" (according to reports).
- "Hundreds of Indians" is safe: Dhruva had about 300 staff when acquired and Rockstar India reportedly about 500 after; headcount figures beyond that are unverified and left out.
- "Secret job" = Rockstar's projects are confidential; no claim beyond that.
- Avoid the reported crunch / overtime allegations: unverified and not this story.
- If posted after 19 November, change line 9 to: "ഇപ്പോൾ, ലോകമെങ്ങുമുള്ള ഗെയിമർമാർ ആ ബീച്ചിലൂടെ വണ്ടിയോടിക്കുന്നു."
