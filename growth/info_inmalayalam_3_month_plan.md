# info.inmalayalam: 3-month plan, 1 → 1,000 followers

Built on the 13 skills from `jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill` (MIT).
Nothing here is posted automatically. You review and post every item.

## Honest starting point

- Now: 1 follower, 1 video.
- 1,000 followers in 3 months is a stretch goal, not a promise. It needs about 1 reel a day with some luck. Treat every week as a test.
- Money: 1,000 followers will not pay you much directly. Instagram's own monetisation generally needs far more followers than that, and I haven't verified current thresholds. Plan for the audience first, then money from the channels that open up later (brand deals, affiliates, a paid course or a Telegram/WhatsApp community, or ad revenue on other platforms). Hitting 1K is the first milestone, not the payday.

## What we know works for this page

- **Malayalam Kerala history, "did you know" facts.** Malayalis share content that makes them proud of Kerala or surprises them. Muziris, the Travancore reforms and the Kerala "firsts" are strong.
- **World facts with a Kerala or Indian hook.** Iron pillar, the Roman gold, the ping-pong diplomacy story (with an Indian hook where it fits).
- **Short (25–40 s) reels with a hook in the first 2 seconds.** Watch-through matters more than the follow button.
- **Share bait, not follow bait.** You asked us to drop "follow" from scripts, so the share line should be "send this to a friend who loves Kerala", not "follow for more".

## What the skills are for

| skill | how we use it here |
| --- | --- |
| `/ig-plan` | Every Monday: pick 4–5 posts. At least 3 reels. Mix proof, teach, opinion, story. |
| `/ig-viral` | Monthly: collect 6–12 Malayalam and Kerala-history accounts, rank by outlier multiple, build the swipe file. Raw views are not evidence. |
| `/ig-reel` | Hook formulas + beat sheet for each reel before we make the video. |
| `/ig-caption` | Check the first 125 characters, max five hashtags. |
| `/ig-profile` | Score the profile now and fix the 12 items in order (name field, first bio line, pinned posts first). |
| `/ig-audit` | After every 10 posts: which ones beat their own account's median. |
| `/ig-human` | Strip AI slop from captions before posting. Use with care: our Malayalam is not English, so check the output by hand. |
| `/ig-comment`, `/ig-reply` | Daily 20 minutes: comment on 10 Malayalam and Kerala-history posts with a real reply, not "🔥". |

**Caveat:** the scoring tools (`hookscore.py`, `beats.py`, `caption.py`, `detect.py`) were written for English. Their hook and slop scores will be unreliable for Malayalam. We judge the Malayalam by reading it, and we can use the tools only for structure (length, timing, number of hashtags).

## 90-day milestones

- **Days 1–30:** 30+ reels posted. Learn which 3 formulas beat their own median. Target: 150 followers.
- **Days 31–60:** Double down on the top formula. Start a series ("Kerala you didn't know"). Target: 400 followers.
- **Days 61–90:** Collaborations with 3–5 small Malayalam pages (shout-outs, not paid). Target: 1,000 followers.

## Rules we keep

- No bought followers, no follow-for-follow farms, no mass follow/unfollow bots. They get accounts restricted.
- Every fact checked against sources before it goes in a script (we already do this).
- Never post a video the owner hasn't approved.
- Track the numbers weekly in `growth/log.md`: reach, watch-through, shares, follows.
