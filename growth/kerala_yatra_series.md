# Kerala Yatra: series format (owner's direction, 22 Nov 2026)

A travel-guide series: one district a day, all 14 districts of Kerala in road order, then the next state. **Not a vlog, and not the explainer reels' hook-twist pattern.** The voice is a calm narrator and navigator, in the spirit of a travel documentary: it tells us where we are, how we get to the next place, what we see there, why it matters, and one practical tip.

## Voice and script rules

- Malayalam narration in first person plural: "നമ്മൾ", "നമ്മുടെ യാത്ര". Calm and warm, like a guide sitting next to you; no shock lines, no "you won't believe", no twist.
- Never say we are foreigners or tourists; we are simply arriving and seeing the place.
- Every stop follows the same four beats: **how we get there** (mode + time), **name**, **what we see**, **one fact or tip** (timing, dress code, closed day, best way up).
- Episode shape: (1) episode 1 only: Earth -> India -> Kerala -> "14 districts" -> landing; later episodes open on the map where the last one ended ("കൊല്ലത്ത് നിന്ന് നമ്മുടെ യാത്ര തുടരുന്നു"). (2) Five stops in road order. (3) Handoff: "... ഇവിടെ തീരുന്നു. നമ്മുടെ യാത്ര തുടരുന്നു... വടക്കോട്ട്, <next district>-ലേക്ക്."
- About 130-140 words, synced to about 70 s.
- Same research rules as everything else: sources kept, real distances (OSRM on OpenStreetMap), nothing invented, fares left out (they change).

## Screen rules

- Opening card: "KERALA YATRA · EP n/14 · DISTRICT".
- A dark navigation-style map on real roads; the route draws with a moving marker that matches the transport (taxi, auto, KSRTC bus, train, bike); a chip with distance, time and mode.
- At each stop: the pin drops, a photo card pops up (number, name, one line), using free-licence Wikimedia Commons photos, credited in the description.
- English place names and chips on screen; Malayalam captions for the voice.
