## Accuracy notes

- **"Half of our cylinders":** the IEA figure is about cylinders for home cooking in India as a whole (not Kerala specifically). Another IEA note says about two-thirds of LPG consumed in India in 2025 transited Hormuz; the script uses the cylinder figure as stated.
- **39 km:** the most-cited narrowest width (21 nautical miles). Some outlets say 33 km (21 statute miles); the script uses 39.
- **Who attacked:** reports say the IRGC Navy claimed explosions on an unnamed gas carrier; the script says only "attacked twice", no attacker named, because reports differ on the weapon (missile vs explosions).
- **Missing seafarer:** latest reports found (18 Nov) say one Indian is still missing; re-check the MEA before posting and change the line if he is found. Crew counts differ slightly across outlets (24 Indians of 25 crew).
- **Ten days:** the IEA says "just over ten days"; the voice says "about ten days".
- **Navy:** Operation Urja Suraksha began in March 2026; the voice says the warships "went out" (past), without claiming it is still running today.
- **Visuals:** no faces of the crew, no images of injured people; the 3D ship is a generic LPG carrier, not a model of Tara Gas.
