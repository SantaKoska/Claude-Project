## Accuracy notes

- Not named: he was 17 when the leak happened. No mention of his medical condition.
- The Fire Stick + phone + hotel TV detail came from one source in this search, so the script says "റിപ്പോർട്ടുകൾ പ്രകാരം" (according to reports).
- "About ninety" videos: sources say about 90 clips (one says about 90 minutes); the script says "തൊണ്ണൂറോളം" (around ninety).
- Left out: the "$5 million cost" figure (one source only), the phishing-email method (single insider claim), and 2026 reports that he is out of hospital and facing a retrial (secondary reporting, unconfirmed).
- "Before a single frame had been officially shown": Rockstar had confirmed the next GTA was in development in February 2022 but released no footage until the December 2023 trailer.
- If posted after 19 November, change line 9 to: "നാല് വർഷം കഴിഞ്ഞു. ഇപ്പോൾ, ആ ഗെയിം ഒടുവിൽ പുറത്തിറങ്ങി."
