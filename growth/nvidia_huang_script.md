# Video 19: One man walked back in... and a company gained about $1 trillion in a day (Nvidia, Jensen Huang)

**Our way:** small cause -> giant effect in the first breath (one man walks back into his office; that one day, Nvidia's value jumps by about one trillion dollars). Then how: 1993, three engineers start Nvidia, Huang CEO for 33 years, the AI boom runs on its chips; 12 November 2026, the affair reports and the China-spy suspicion, he resigns, the value falls from over $4 trillion to about $3 trillion in 11 days. Silence. Twist: 23 November, the board takes him back, and the man behind the deal is the US President. Payoff: +31% in one day, the price Wall Street put on one man; "a better CEO the second time"; Congress still waiting. Callback to the man walking back in.
**Why it happened (the owner's question), in one line:** investors and the US government decided Nvidia without Huang was worth about $1 trillion less, and the government said it saw no security risk.
**Emotion:** "wait, what?" (11 days, out and back), awe at the number.
**Share reason:** the biggest business story of the week; a quick "what actually happened" to send to friends. (No CTA.)
**Hook card (0-3 s):** ഒരാൾ തിരിച്ചെത്തി... $1 ട്രില്യൺ!

## Accuracy notes

- **"About one trillion dollars":** Fortune rounds to $1 trillion; CNN says $952 billion. The voice says "ഏകദേശം" (about); the screen shows "+$950 BN" in the payoff.
- **The fall:** over $4 trillion before 12 Nov (Bloomberg: it fell *below* $4 trillion that day) to about $3 trillion at the Friday 20 Nov close (Fortune). 12 -> 23 Nov is 11 days.
- **The scandal is told as reports, not as fact:** Huang admitted infidelity and apologised (Al Jazeera). The China-spy link is what US intelligence officials believe, per reports; no one has been charged. The women are not named or shown. Congress committees still plan a closed-door interview on 1 Dec.
- **Trump's role:** Trump said he made the deal (CNBC); the board said the US government gave input. The voice says only "behind it, Trump". The JD Vance detail is in one report only, so it is not used.
- **"33 years, one CEO":** CEO since founding in 1993 (Nvidia bio); the 11-day interim CEO (Kress) is the only exception, so the line is about his 33 years before the resignation.
- **ChatGPT line:** OpenAI's models are trained on Nvidia GPUs (widely reported; Nvidia-OpenAI partnership). No market-share number is used.
- **Logo and photo use:** Nvidia logo and press photos of Huang are shown for news reporting with credit; no images of the women involved.
