# Visual plan: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi 204 off 53

**Idea:** one set, one transformation. A golden-hour farm plot (the land his father sold) becomes the 22-yard pitch of a floodlit stadium that rises out of the ground, then turns back into the farm at the end.

**Resources:** reused our own kit (lib/anatomy3d.js Studio + Exploded, the nvidia.html page skeleton with captions, pills, labels). New world: lib/stadium3d.js. GitHub search not repeated: the cricket/stadium needs were covered by our existing three.js kit and the 1983 cricket ball code; no outside assets, so no licence questions.

**Look (not cartoon):** real proportions (22 m pitch, 0.711 m stumps, 7.2 cm ball), furrowed soil with bump, fine crop blades with colour variation, hazy distant tree line in golden fog, mown outfield stripes, tiered stands with seat colours, four floodlight towers with bloom, LED scoreboard with a pixel grid, soft shadows + AO + DOF.
