# Split EP1 (₹15 tea): visual plan

Owner, 26 Nov 2026: "the best video so far", using the editing, colour and layout pattern of the business-breakdown reels he shared (brandology.io screenshots), because that look grew a page to about 15k followers in 10 videos. We take the *pattern*, not their assets: no copied art, logo, title text or characters.

## The series look (applies to every split episode)
- Light white tile wall (a real Kerala tea-shop wall), sky strip with palm leaves over the wall cap, wooden counter. Bright from frame 0.
- Titles: small Malayalam line in Anek Malayalam 800 + the key word on a lime (#C8F53A) highlight bar.
- Information as graphics: navy (#141A3C) cards and pills, lime / navy "+ ITEM ₹price" pills popping in, a meter board for volume.
- Captions: navy rounded box, Baloo Chettan 2 800, karaoke word highlight in lime, money words underlined in lime.
- One real photo per episode in a white frame, credited (Wikimedia Commons).
- Transitions: horizontal slide between scenes (the wall pans with them), whoosh on each.
- Fonts are OFL (fonts/OFL_*.txt), from the npm fontsource packages.
