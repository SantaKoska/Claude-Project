# Video 8: The night the doorbell rang (Gardner Museum heist, 1990)

Emotion: suspense, then the chill of an unsolved mystery.
Note: sources call this the biggest ART theft in history; the script says that, not "biggest theft of anything".

## English, by beat

1. COLD OPEN: 18 March 1990. Past midnight. At the door of a museum in Boston... the bell rang.
2. PERSON (the visitors): Outside, two policemen. They said they had come to check on a problem.
3. PERSON: Inside on duty, a 23-year-old guard. He opened the door.
4. PROBLEM: The next moment... the guards were in handcuffs, tied up in the basement.
5. TURN: Because those two men... were not police.
6. RISING: They walked among some of the most valuable paintings in the world. Rembrandt... Vermeer...
7. RISING: Thirteen works of art. Some cut straight out of their frames.
8. PAYOFF: About 500 million dollars... the biggest art theft in history.
9. PAYOFF: 36 years have passed. A 10-million-dollar reward. And still... not one painting has come back.
10. CALLBACK: Even today, on those walls, the empty frames still hang... a memory of the bell that rang that night.

## Visual note per beat (2D documentary style)

1. Night, rain, a grand old museum door lit by one lamp; a doorbell glows; "18.03.1990 · ബോസ്റ്റൺ" typewriter stamp.
2. Two silhouettes in police caps through the door's glass, a police badge glint.
3. Young guard at a desk with CCTV monitors and a buzzer button; his finger presses it.
4. Hard cut: handcuffs click (sound), a basement corridor, a flickering bulb.
5. Freeze: the two caps fall away, revealing plain dark silhouettes. Music drops.
6. Slow track along a dark gallery wall: gilded frames with drawn storm-at-sea and music-room paintings (our own drawings, not reproductions).
7. A knife line traces around a canvas inside its frame; a counter "1 → 13".
8. Gold counter to "$500,000,000", red stamp "ചരിത്രത്തിലെ ഏറ്റവും വലിയ കലാമോഷണം".
9. Calendar years flip 1990 → 2026; a reward poster "$10,000,000"; "0 തിരിച്ചുകിട്ടി".
10. The same gallery today: empty gilded frames on the wall, the doorbell glows once more, fade.

## Accuracy notes

- "$500 million" is the usual estimate; at the time it was put at $200 million and a 2026 report says over $1 billion today. The script says "about 500 million".
- The guard who opened the door, Rick Abath, was long a suspect but was never charged and denied involvement until his death in 2024. The script only says he opened the door, not that he was involved.
- The disguised men claiming to respond to a disturbance, and the guards being handcuffed and tied in the basement, are the standard account (Wikipedia, History.com); the exact 81-minute duration was not confirmed, so it is left out.
- The paintings in the visuals must be our own drawings, not reproductions of the stolen works.
