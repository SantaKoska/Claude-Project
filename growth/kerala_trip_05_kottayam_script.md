# Kerala Yatra, episode 5/14: Kottayam

**Style:** narrator and navigator (see the `kerala-yatra` skill). Calm "നമ്മൾ" voice; each stop = how we get there -> name -> what we see -> one fact.
**Language:** Malayalam voice; English names, distances and chips on screen.
**Length:** 119 Malayalam words with the series intro (EP 3: 124 words = 66.9 s synced), so about 78 s raw, synced to about 65 s.
**Opening:** the space intro (Earth -> India -> Kerala -> 14 districts; districts 1-4 done, Kottayam highlighted), then down to Kuttanad, where episode 4 ended on the houseboat.
**Opening card:** KERALA YATRA · EP 5/14 · KOTTAYAM. Chip: LETTERS · LATEX · LAKES.
**Thread:** Kottayam's three Ls. The lake (Kumarakom), then letters (Vaikom's fight to walk the road, the old college, the literacy town), then the hills where the rubber country rises towards Idukki.

## Accuracy notes

- **Illikkal Kallu height:** sources give 3,400, 3,500, 3,600 and 6,000 ft, so the voice and chips give no height.
- **Kumarakom size:** 14 acres (Wikipedia, most sources); some pages say 14 hectares. Not used in the voice.
- **Vaikom:** "for everyone's right to walk" summarises the ban on so-called lower castes using the temple roads. The date 1924 is the start (it ran to November 1925). No temple timings or dress code in the voice (not checked).
- **CMS College:** the college says 1817 (Wikipedia's infobox once said 1815), so the voice says "more than 200 years" and the chip says 1817. Not called "India's first college" (sources disagree).
- **Literacy:** sources say town, city or district; Wikipedia and the municipality say the town/city, 1989. The voice says "town".
- **Alphonsamma:** "India's first woman saint" = first woman of Indian origin canonised (2008). Feast 19-28 July (not in the voice).
- **Times:** bus times rounded up from OSRM drive times (18, 35, 33 min). No fares. The taxi climb and the boat crossing have no time.
- **Left out:** Ettumanoor and Thirunakkara temples, Vagamon (mostly Idukki), Aruvikkuzhi falls.
